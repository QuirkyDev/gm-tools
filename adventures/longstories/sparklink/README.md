## Session notes

| Session | Title | Summary | Session file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Prisoners of the Underdark | The group learns about their drow captors, the other prisoners, and the dangers of the prison. Ouus secretly acquires drow poison, and Vaeril overhears a conversation about political struggles. | [Session notes](notes/session-1.md) |
| 2 | Northbound | The group chooses the northern route, argues over the anti-magic field item, finds a map marked with an X, and Artemas is ambushed by a ghost. | [Session notes](notes/session-2.md) |
| 3 | The Gnoll Chase | The group helps Bob the bard escape wild gnolls and agrees to seek the Garden of Plenty with him. Artemas is weakened by a ghost-like creature, and Ouus offers to carry his shield and crowbar. | [Session notes](notes/session-3.md) |
| 4 | Gnolls in the Dark | The group explores the caves, defeats several gnolls, and encounters Duergar herding fire beetles at a fork. Vaeril scouts for hostiles, and Ouus attacks. | [Session notes](notes/session-4.md) |
| 5 | Entering the Garden of Plenty | The group reaches the Garden of Plenty, explores its ruined temple, and learns about possible routes toward the surface. After eating and drinking, they feel high; Vaeril later sees strange figures approaching the temple. | [Session notes](notes/session-5.md) |
| 6 | Echoes of the Six Sisters | The group discovers zombies emerging from the temple and enters its dungeon, where murals tell of six sisters battling fiery invaders and sealing a rift. | [Session notes](notes/session-6.md) |
| 7 | The Six Awakened | Undead emerge from the statues and coffin; the group defeats them, but the leader kills Artemas. Ouus takes the inscribed sword, and Amanita claims seven flame-marked rings. | [Session notes](notes/session-7.md) |
| 8 | Leaving the Garden | The group leaves the Garden of Plenty with an Illusion Stone and learns of a druid who may be able to revive Artemas. Ouus snaps Vaeril out of the feeling that he should never leave. | [Session notes](notes/session-8.md) |
| 9 | A Night at the Drowsy Puffball | The group leaves the Garden, encounters slimes, and stays at the Drowsy Puffball. Vaeril borrows money from Ulric, and the group hears rumors about Blingdenstone. | [Session notes](notes/session-9.md) |
| 10 | Meet Bradluc and Voc | The group meets Bradluc and his steel defender, Voc, learns that the pin is a House Vandree insignia, and discovers a gnoll shrine and an underground oasis with divinatory visions. | [Session notes](notes/session-10.md) |
