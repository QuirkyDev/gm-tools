| 12 | The city with two fishy shamans | The group follows a new Kuo-Toa faction to Sloobludop, hears conflicting accounts from two shamans, and learns they may be sacrificed to summon Leemooggoogoon. | [Session notes](notes/session-12.md) |
| 14 | The Druid's Cure | The group finds Caelith, a sick druid, and learns that Spellsip Suckers are needed to make her antidote. Ouus and Voc help catch the leeches. | [Session notes](notes/session-14.md) |
| 15 | The Return of Skygge | Caelith's ritual revives Artemas, who awakens with amnesia and claims the name Skygge. The group chooses a route toward Gracklstugh and defeats a band of harpies. | [Session notes](notes/session-15.md) |

| 19 | The Malformed Giant | The Stone Guard joins the group against an out-of-control stone giant, and Dahrun explains his friend Rihuus's condition. The group heads to the Gable Shack to find Ylsa. | [Session notes](notes/session-19.md) |
