# The Shattered Spire's Offer

## The Shattered Spire

The Iron Stitch sells hide armor for 10 gp, but Amanita was broke. We headed to the Shattered Spire, a broken stalagmite rising out of the water. It was quiet, with older people drinking mushroom coffee and younger people who seemed to still be drunk.

Werz gave us a chest containing four crystals as thanks for helping him. He said some of the gems were stolen: they are obsidian gems, not especially rare, worth around 5-10 gp each.

Ulric asked about getting to the world above. Werz said he needed a favor and had a client in Blingdenstone who would pay the rest. Deep gnomes are rebuilding and reclaiming the city, which is well hidden and not widely known. Werz warned us not to tell anyone because it could endanger them. He gave us a small chest of essential materials to help rebuild the city.

When Ulric asked how this would get us to the surface, Werz explained that the delivery would bring us near a passage upward and that he could point us to a guide. Ylsa Henstak often leads trading caravans and can usually be found with one somewhere in the Blade Bazaar. The reward is a pouch of 60 gp, plus 140 gp when we deliver the chest to Kazook, a deep gnome in Blingdenstone.

Werz also told us that deep gnomes ride lizards and Duergar ride giant spiders.

## What we wanted to be

Over a drink, Ulric asked what we wanted to become as children, whether we had come close, and whether it was still our dream.

- Ouus wanted to become the village guard. He feels he succeeded and accomplished his dream.
- Ulric wanted to earn enough to live comfortably and help other orphans.
- Amanita took out a viol that might have belonged to the body's previous owner. Amanita feels young in some way and does not yet have a big dream, beyond growing stronger and reaching the surface.
- Skygge was quiet at first, then said the idea of individual dreams was strange. Skygge lives for the clan and its goals, not personal goals. Skygge's purpose is to follow the Raven Queen, find her artifacts, and make her stronger.

## Trouble in the Blade Bazaar

Two shady figures made racist remarks and became aggressive. When they touched Amanita, Amanita hit them with a stick, and a fight began. After taking some hits, they fled.

Amanita returned to the Iron Stitch, bought the 10 gp hide armor, and sold the old armor.

While looking for Ylsa, we heard very heavy footsteps. A giant form stepped from behind a building: a stone giant with a malformed second head. It stormed toward us.
