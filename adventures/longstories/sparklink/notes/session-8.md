# Leaving the Garden

## Warnings about drugs

We were warned not to sell drugs in Cracle Stug(?) or near the Duergar. Drugs are illegal, and the Duergar disapprove of their sale.

## A possible way to revive Artemas

There is a druid at Timberbark Grove who may be able to revive Artemas.

## Leaving the Garden of Plenty

After failing a Wisdom saving throw, Vaeril began to feel as though he never wanted to leave the Garden of Plenty. Vaeril was being a huge pain in the ass, but Ouus finally snapped him out of it.

As we left, the dwarves gave us an Illusion Stone.
