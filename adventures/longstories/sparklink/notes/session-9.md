# A Night at the Drowsy Puffball

## On the way to Timberbark Grove

We left the Garden of Plenty and headed toward Timberbark Grove. In a cave, we encountered slimes. Using a slime ability, Amanita spoke with them telepathically. The slimes seemed to believe Amanita was their "master" (uncertain) and asked whether Amanita needed more of them. Amanita said no more were needed and told them to wait one day, after which they would go to Blingdenstone.

We heard that Blingdenstone was attacked by Drow about 200 years ago. There is also a rumor that someone—possibly Duergar—tried to reclaim it.

## The Drowsy Puffball

We continued on toward an inn called the Drowsy Puffball. Its innkeeper, Luzza, is a mushroom person. The inn hides itself from evil creatures and is visible only to good or neutral creatures.

Vaeril borrowed seven Drossle from Ulric to pay for the night. Vaeril was advised to visit the big cities to look for the missing person.

## Rest and trivia

Some mushrooms glow during daylight and stop glowing at night.

We took a long rest and then left the inn.
