# The Malformed Giant

## The Stone Guard and Rihuus

We tried to stop the stone giant. A couple of Duergar from the Stone Guard appeared out of invisibility and helped us. They said stone giants are not normally this aggressive. They recognized us as outsiders and asked what we were doing in the city. Ouus intimidated them into showing us more respect by pointing out that we had just saved the area from an out-of-control giant.

The guards invited us to visit Overlake Hold, the Stone Guard's base of operations and a temple to Laduguer. They pointed out a stalagmite where it is located.

We examined the giant's body and found purple ooze in its mouth that seemed to come from a disease. While Ouus tried to remove a tooth as a trophy, another giant approached calmly and asked what we were doing. The giant introduced himself as Dahrun, the Big Friendly Giant (BFG), and said the dead giant was his friend Rihuus (also spelled Rihhuut in our notes). Rihuus had been talking to the stones and performing a ritual when he went crazy.

When Dahrun asked why Ouus had taken a tooth from his friend, Ouus convinced him that he planned to have a doctor examine it. Ouus asked Dahrun to tell us something good about Rihuus. Dahrun said Rihuus was kind, religious, and an apprentice Stone Speaker. The Stone Speaker Hgraam may know what to do; we can find Hgraam in the Caregorn Caverns. Dahrun asked us not to vilify the stone giants. We said goodbye and left to look for Ylsa.

## The Gable Shack

There was no caravan at the Gable Shack; the DM retconned that detail. Bouncers gave us a once-over as we entered. Inside, games included roulette, two kinds of card games, and two kinds of dice games. Skygge looked over the games without trying to be addressed.

Amanita went to the bar. A friendly Duergar asked what they could get us. Amanita asked for Ylsa, explained that we needed help traveling somewhere, and ordered drinks. We entered the back room, where the session ended.

Ouus has two pending actions: one involving Skygge and one involving Ouus's beer.
