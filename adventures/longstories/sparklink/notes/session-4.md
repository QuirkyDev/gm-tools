# Gnolls in the Dark

## Exploring the caves

Vaeril explored part of the cave and heard sounds that might have come from hostiles.

The group also explored a narrow cave passage. At the end, we found a warm, dug-out hole containing two eggs. We left them undisturbed.

As we continued through the partly explored caves, Vaeril pointed out the possible hostile locations. Ouus went on the offensive, and we killed several gnolls, though their leader escaped. We looted some gems and found cooked food.

## The northern fork

We took the northern exit and followed the path until we reached a fork. There, we encountered two Duergar riding a Carrion Crawler and herding hundreds of fire beetles.
