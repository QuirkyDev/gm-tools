# Echoes of the Six Sisters

## The zombies

The creatures were zombies that came from the temple. They wore armor resembling the armor shown in the temple murals, and each had a tattoo that looked like a goat's skull.

Vaeril wanted to keep eating, so we waited until the next day before proceeding and closed up for the night. (OOC: Vaeril wanted to recover resources.) Ouus told the princess about the zombies, but she did not care because she was too high. The Duergar seemed concerned: they inspected the bodies, panicked, and went to check on everything.

Amanita and Artemas kept watch over the place where the zombies had come from. We took a long rest. The princess was still sleeping off the previous day, so we could not inform her further.

## The dungeon and its murals

We entered the dungeon, which has a Gothic style with red as a key color. In the first room, we found murals with old Elvish text beneath them, written in a dialect.

### First mural

The mural shows a portal with creatures emerging from it. The creatures appear to have features of fire, coal, or lava.

> From rift of flame, the invaders came,  
> A burning tide, none could tame.  
> Caverns wept with molten glow,  
> Doom descending on drow below.

### Second mural

On the left, armored Drow fight the creatures, but the battle is going poorly. In the middle, a Drow holds a sword with a blue glow—the only blue in the painting. On the right, the battle appears to be going better.

> Six sisters bold and a leader wise,  
> Met flame with steel beneath the skies.  
> A fiery lord they sought to bind,  
> Through cunning craft and steely mind.

### Third mural

The fire creatures flee into the portal as it is sealed. The Drow armor appears scorched. The leader holds a glowing, ember-like crystal.

> With ward and blade, the tide was turned,  
> The rift was sealed, the flames unburned.  
> Six sisters rest, their duty done,  
> In shadowed halls, their names live on.
