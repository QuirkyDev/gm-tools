# New Hires at Gundren’s Lab

## The New Guys

We changed our plan and surrounded the Duergar woman to intimidate her. She asked if we were the new guys. Ouus answered truthfully. “Yeah, we're very new here.” She shooed us away.

The room was full of mushrooms. Ouus asked what we were supposed to be doing, and we asked if the boss was around. She said he was away. She helped him with the alchemy, and he sold the potions.

Then she saw the bodies of the Duergar and panicked. When she tried to run, Ouus struck her with a shield to try to knock her out. Skygge cast *Hold Person*, and we tied her up.

Her name was Lorthia Bukbukken. Her boss, Gundren, ran the underground laboratory and had taught her alchemy. Our questions got us little further. Lorthia pointed to the desk and said the papers there would tell us what we needed to know.

## Gundren's Desk

Amanita found the accounts on the desk. Someone had crossed out the names, but she could still read them by holding the pages up to the light. In a hidden compartment, she found a letter marked “For Gundren.”

> I don’t need your poison anymore. I’ll deal with Werz Saltbaron myself. Bring me an elf blade, one with the swirlies carved on the steel, and I’ll forget you failed me. And I don’t want to see any of your goons near my post. The captain is poking around, and I could use a scapegoat.
>
> - Gorglak

Lorthia told us Gorglak was one of the guards. We also asked her about Droakie. As far as she knew, he was just a supplier. He rarely spoke, and when he did, he rambled. She had no way to contact him. We got the impression that Lorthia had been so absorbed in her work that she'd missed what Gundren was up to.

## Back to Gracklstugh

We kidnapped Lorthia and took her with us. Ouus approached a door with peepholes at dwarf height. Beyond it, a ladder led up to a wooden cover. Ouus heard a scraping sound from above. He lifted the cover and looked out over the Dark Lake. The sound he'd heard was waves breaking nearby. We climbed up after him, back in Gracklstugh.
