# The city with two fishy shamans

## Along the Dark Lake

While walking beside the lake, Vaeril saw movement beneath the water at least 10 metres from shore (Perception 13). The Kuo-Toa walked close to the water and did not seem bothered by it. Bradluc listened to the Kuo-Toa as we walked (Perception 20). They made small talk but avoided discussing something in particular.

We met another group of Kuo-Toa with beliefs like Shuushar's. Bradluc spoke with them and managed to avoid conflict. We left the first group and followed the second group to their city. The second group said the first group knew only violence. The first group still wanted us to speak with their shaman, Bloblipoop. The second group also wanted us to meet their shaman, Plooploopeen, so we could hear their side of the story.

On the way, we passed a statue used for blood sacrifices to Leemooggoogoon.

## Sloobludop

We found lodging near a statue of Blibdoolpoolp. The statue smelled foul and had more traditional offerings, including gold, fish, and seaweed.

A Duergar named Hemeth had gone missing. Plooploopeen suspected that followers of Blibdoolpoolp had kidnapped and sacrificed Hemeth, who had indeed vanished.

Plooploopeen proposed giving us as a peace offering, apparently as a cover to kill the other faction's shaman. The notes name Plooploopeen as the intended target, which does not make sense given the surrounding details; the intended target is unclear. The plan also seemed inconsistent with their claim to be peaceful, especially since the sacrificial altar was in public view. We decided to speak with Bloblipoop before doing anything.

Before meeting Bloblipoop, we visited the shop Gurgling Bargains. Each item cost 300 doublin. The shop sold:

- A clam of confessions (bought): it opens when someone tells a lie.
- Shell phones (bought), similar to sending stones.
- A bubble of holding, which can carry up to 50 lb.
- A hardened crab-shell necklace called a Crustian Charm, which can cast *Sanctuary* once per day.

## Bloblipoop and the prophecy

We saw a Duergar in chains. Bloblipoop said the Duergar was also part of the prophecy but was unwilling, which was why they were chained. Bloblipoop is Plooploopeen's daughter.

Bloblipoop said that all of us would be sacrificed to summon Leemooggoogoon. They seemed fanatical. They planned to sacrifice the Duergar at 14:00 the following day.

Plooploopeen described the divine tenets of Blibdoolpoolp, though their account may be biased:

- Kuo-Toa are her children.
- She ensures safe sea travel.
- She protects her children throughout their lives.
