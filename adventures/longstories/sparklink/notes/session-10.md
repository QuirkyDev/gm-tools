# Meet Bradluc and Voc

## The rubble pile and the gnolls

We found a pin made of dark metal and gold lying on the ground. None of us could identify it, so Amanita picked it up.

Later, at a fork in the road, three gnolls searched behind a pile of stone rubble. They were aggressive, but not toward us. Beneath the rubble was a deep gnome with blue-purple skin and a white mohawk. Ouus decided to attack the gnolls, and combat began. During the fight, the rubble pile started moving: it was a steel defender, like a golem. The deep gnome joined the fight against the gnolls.

The deep gnome introduced himself as Bradluc Slatesmelter. Bradluc does not speak Common, but can communicate with Amanita in Elvish and also speaks Dwarvish. The steel defender is named Voc. Bradluc and Voc are also heading toward Timberbark Grove. Amanita said that we were looking for a cure for our friend Artemas. Bradluc is looking for a cure for Drow poison.

Bradluc recognized the pin as an insignia of House Vandree, the house name of some of our captors. Asha, the priestess, and Shoor, the lieutenant, wore the same insignia.

## The gnoll shrine

We came across another pile of rocks, this one arranged like an exotic, jungle-like shrine with a skull. The skull was not fresh and appeared animal-like rather than human. We heard a hyena-like cackle. The hyena skull had a socket for a third eye.

Ulric confirmed that there are no hyena-like creatures with a third eye above ground. Ouus sensed that evil rituals had been cast there, and Bradluc recognized it as a gnoll shrine. We proceeded with caution.

## The underground oasis

We entered a larger, circular chamber with stalactites and a pool of water in the center, like an underground oasis. When we looked into the water, we did not see our own reflections. Instead, each of us saw someone we love or have another strong emotional connection to.

- Amanita saw a ten-year-old human girl. Amanita felt deep love but did not recognize the girl or know their relationship. Amanita reached into the water, but nothing happened.
- Bradluc saw the woman Bradluc is dating—their relationship is a "situationship," with nothing concrete. Bradluc cast *Detect Magic* and sensed divination magic.
- Ouus briefly saw Artemas, but the vision quickly changed into something powerful and unfamiliar. Ouus turned away.
- Ulric saw guards approaching and also turned away.

Ulric would rather have left, but we spent the night there. Amanita spent the night staring into the water. Bradluc cast *Alarm*.
