# The Return of Skygge

## Caelith's antidote and resurrection ritual

We returned to the hut and gave Caelith two Spellsip Suckers. She began making the antidote and needed another night's rest before she would recover, so we stayed at the hut.

In the morning, Caelith was preparing a resurrection ritual after Celyn told her about it. The ritual had to take place elsewhere. Bradluc asked Celyn for a recipe for an antidote to Drow poison.

Caelith led us to a mushroom circle surrounding a stone altar overgrown with roots. She began the ritual, and Artemas awakened. He seemed to have amnesia. The last thing he remembered was seeking treasure for the Raven Queen: a knife. He said his name was Skygge. We may have brought back someone who had inhabited Artemas's body before.

Amanita recognized a similarity to their own experience: Amanita believes they did not inhabit this body before waking as Amanita. Caelith sensed Amanita's essence and confirmed that Amanita is their own person, likely a spore servant who gained sentience, with the previous host no longer present.

Ouus asked Caelith where Artemas was. She said that whatever had controlled Skygge's body had now been cleansed. Ouus was very unsettled by these revelations.

## Choosing a route

Skygge agreed to join us in searching for a way out of the Underdark (not the Abyss). Caelith suggested two routes upward: Gracklstugh, the Duergar city, whose people know the best passages, or a passage through Mantol-Derith that she knows and that leads higher into the Underdark. Gracklstugh offers more options. We agreed to drop Bradluc off on the way to Gracklstugh.

## The harpies

On the way, we heard singing. Ouus, Bradluc, and Skygge were charmed; Amanita and Ulric were not. Amanita tried to bonk Ouus on the head but failed. Ulric tried to counter the charm but also failed.

Combat broke out with the harpies. We killed them all. Two tried to escape, but none survived.
