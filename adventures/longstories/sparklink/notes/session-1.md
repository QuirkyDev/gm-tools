# Prisoners of the Underdark

## The drow and their servants

We were kidnapped approximately eight or nine days ago. We now know that a drow assault was responsible. There appear to be about three or four drow warriors, based on their clothing, and around two Quaggoths serving the drow. A Quaggoth is a humanoid creature that walks on all fours and has wolf-like features.

## Rumours about the drow

The surnames mentioned below are clan names.

- Ilvara Mizzrym runs the operation and is a high priestess of Lolth.
- Asha Vandree is an assistant priestess.
- Shoor Vandree is the commander or lieutenant, and is currently Ilvara's lover.
- Jorlan Duskryn was Ilvara's former lover. He was badly wounded fighting an ooze, which severely damaged his face; this is said to be why Ilvara ended their relationship.
- There is something in the water that the drow call "The Thing."

## Fellow prisoners

- Shuushar is a Kuo-Toa who could guide us safely to his village.
- Buppido is a Derro (described as a dwarf/gnome combination). He talks a lot and could lead us to safety at a Duergar outpost ruled by the Deep King.
- Eldeth is a dwarf who could take us to a safe place where deep gnomes live.
- Ront was a half-orc. After Ilvara tortured him with her tentacle rod, he was pushed off a cliff.

## The prison and recent events

There is an anti-magic field in our cell. It seems to originate from the gate outside.

During the most recent work detail, Ouus stole drow poison from Ilvara's room and concealed how he did it.

Vaeril overheard Shoor and Ilvara speaking. A woman in the conversation said she did not want to be there, did not understand why her father had put her there, and believed the situation was connected to political struggles. The notes do not make clear which woman this refers to.
