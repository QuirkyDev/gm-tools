# The Druid's Cure

## Caelith's condition

We arrived at Timberbark Grove and encountered a hungry mushroom hippo, which we killed. We then found a small hut protected by a druidic sigil. A small elven girl named Celyn Fendew answered the door and let us in. Her mother, Caelith, is a druid who looked unwell. She had circular bite marks on her arms and had been bitten by Spellsip Suckers, manavore leeches that feed on magical energy. They hate fire.

To cure Caelith, we need an antidote made from the Spellsip Suckers. Celyn pointed us in the right direction. We left our deceased friend, Artemas, in the hut.

## Hunting the Spellsip Suckers

Ouus took off his chainmail and borrowed leather armor from Celyn; it fit him well.

We went fishing for the leeches with a light-emitting rock tied to a javelin. The leeches came to investigate but eventually lost interest. Voc, the steel defender, and Ouus used themselves as bait. We killed the Spellsip Suckers easily, though Ouus became exhausted.
