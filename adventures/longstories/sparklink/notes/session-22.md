# Balik's Last Request

## The Cave Altar

We entered the cave with one change to last session's account. The DM had prepared a different part of the map, so we retconned the end of the chase. Amanita hadn't seen which way Droakie went.

Bodies lay piled around an altar where a man stood with a knife and a book. He said he wanted to “free” us, then summoned skeletons as the fight began. We killed him and the skeletons with little trouble. During the fight, Ouus accidentally stabbed the ritual book and partly burned it. He handed what was left to Amanita.

**Level 6 reminder:** Amanita has the damaged ritual book. Study it when the party reaches level 6.

## Balik's Request

A ghost named Balik appeared and thanked us. He asked us to take part of his remains back to his family in Blingdenstone. His severed hand had been enchanted and still wore an onyx ring. He gave us the name of his husband, Perrin Underbough.

Ouus asked Balik whether there was anything valuable nearby. We found 10 gp, 11 sp, 12 cp, a ring set with a white crystal, and a piece of lizard-skin parchment. Balik warned us about a Derro in a strange hat. When we asked which way the Derro had gone, Balik ignored the question.

## The Lady's Dream

Farther into the cave, Amanita heard several creatures shuffling in place. We sent her lizard ahead. It found myconids and spore servants that looked like werewolves. They paid Amanita no attention as she approached. When she said hello, one of the myconids answered her telepathically.

He recognized Amanita as a fellow myconid and told her about “the Lady” and the dream she offered. He offered Amanita the dream so the Lady could guide her to her destiny. Amanita declined. She wanted to choose her own path. The myconids understood and let us pass, asking only that we keep their presence secret.

## The Mushroom Garden

We came to a dense mushroom garden. Amanita's lizard spotted swarms of insects and spiders in the middle, so we kept close to the wall and turned right.
