# Entering the Garden of Plenty

## Finding the garden

We found five fire beetles and brought them back to their herders, Zegs and Edsel, two dwarves. They led us to a secret cave. A dead-end wall opened when someone spoke a magical word in Sylvan and closed with another word. Beyond it was the Garden of Plenty.

We met Giggles, an ogre; Suortin, an umber hulk who may be the princess's bodyguard; and Annarei, the princess of the garden.

## Downtime

- Vaeril practiced "Mask of the Wild" and can now use it around mushrooms. Vaeril traded one +1 rope for two torches.
- Amanita went mushroom-picking but stopped early because the activity was disturbing others. Amanita noticed Zerkwood, bluecaps, and whitecaps.
- Ouus sparred with Suortin.

## The ruined temple

A ruined temple lies to the northeast. It contains elvish-like writing that is not Elvish. Vaeril could not read it but copied it down.

Murals carved into the walls depict female figures leaning on long swords; no male figures are shown. Something resembling fire appears in the background. The temple may be connected to an Underdark religion with martial elements, or it may belong to a Drow tribe associated with fire. Annarei also does not know the temple's origins; they think the Drow built it to honor their warrior.

Ouus felt at home in the temple.

## Rumours about the surface routes

The trade routes through Mantol-Derith may provide a way back to the surface, and Blingdenstone is close to Mantol-Derith. Blingdenstone may have been abandoned after being overrun by Drow about 15 years ago; it might now be an outpost.

## The food, wine, and figures

We ate some of the garden's food and drank wine. After a while, we felt high. Ulric spaced out completely; the rest of us were affected but managed. A pouch of drugs was passed around, but none of us took any.

Amanita told the group that they had lost all their memories.

Vaeril heard a sound from the back and went to investigate alone. Vaeril saw strange figures, some apparently missing parts—possibly their heads—approaching the temple. Vaeril called out to Ouus that some of his friends had arrived.
