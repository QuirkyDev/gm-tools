# The Six Awakened

## Names beneath the statues

Amanita, Ouus, and Ulric entered the room. Each rolled 15 on a Perception check and noticed old Elvish text beneath the statues. The text listed their names:

- Leader: Qilvrae Nyzzrath
- Guards:
  - Zilryna Kren’vyl
  - Drisvyra T’lindeth
  - Sharindra Myzzarath
  - Vaylthara Zor’vahl
  - Illyndra Xil’tarath
  - Sylzora Drezzynne

Amanita heard what sounded like someone pounding on a wall with their fists. A loud bang followed, and something broke out of the coffin. Each of the six guard statues then broke open, releasing a zombie.

## The fight and the grave

We fought the undead. The leader emerged from the coffin and killed Artemas with a single hit. The rest of us managed to defeat the remaining hostiles.

Ouus took the sword from the grave. It was inscribed, “I isilrîn le govannen” (“Those who freeze, greet you”). The zombies and mummies had the same goat-skull tattoo as the undead we encountered earlier. Each had an identical ring bearing a flame symbol. Amanita took all seven rings.

Vaeril shouted at the people of the Garden of Plenty, blaming them for Artemas's death. Vaeril was projecting.

## After the fight

We levelled up. Amanita learned *Gentle Repose* and cast it on Artemas.
