# Sailing into Gracklstugh

## Bradluc's departure

We dropped Bradluc off near the Kuo-Toa. Bradluc told us to visit Glamrock Hold if we were ever nearby. It is mostly inhabited by deep gnomes; we should ask for Bradluc or the name of a guild that we do not remember. Bradluc rode Voc, the steel defender, off into the sunset.

## Taking the Duergar ship

Amanita steered our boat onward until we encountered another ship. It looked darker and more imposing, and several Duergar were on deck. They stopped us and demanded that we hand everything over if we wanted to pass.

Ouus threw a bag of burning harpy feathers, and combat began. Amanita accidentally pushed Ouus into the water with *Thunderwave* and felt bad about it. We commandeered the ship and continued onward.

We saw a huge cavern and heard the sounds of a city. As we approached Gracklstugh, the industrial and smithing smog made it difficult to breathe.

## Family, origins, and plans

We talked about our relationships with our fathers and where we came from.

- Ouus does not really know his father; his mother told him about him.
- Ulric never knew a father. Ulric does not know whether his parents died. Ulric was raised in an orphanage and then moved around a lot. Ulric's patron entered the picture more recently.
- Skygge said he is loyal to the Raven Queen. In Skygge's society, clans care for one another instead of having parental relationships. Ouus wants to meet the Raven Queen. Skygge suggested bringing a gift, such as one of her weapons, but needs to find a portal home first.
- Amanita does not know who the parents of the body they inhabit were. Thanks to Caelith, Amanita now knows they have a creator—a myconid—and wants to meet him. When Ouus asked whether Amanita had any memories, Amanita recalled the lake and the young girl seen in its reflection. Amanita thinks it may have been a memory belonging to the body's previous inhabitant, who loved the girl very much. Ouus suggested she might have been the previous inhabitant's mother. Amanita could neither confirm nor deny this, since they do not know what that feels like.
