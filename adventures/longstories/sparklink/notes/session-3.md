# The Gnoll Chase

## Along the river

We defeated the ghost-like creature, but it drained some of Artemas's strength, leaving him unable to carry some of his items. As Artemas's friend, Ouus offered to carry his shield and crowbar.

We continued following the river. There was a lot of noise and falling rocks, caused by nearby creatures. We managed to sneak past them, though some party members were hit by boulders. We healed most of the damage.

## Bob and the Garden of Plenty

We eventually reached a cave system, where a gnome-like bard named Bob ran toward us. Wild gnolls were chasing him and looked ready to eat both Bob and us, so we jumped in to help him.

During the combat, Ouus gained inspiration after rolling a natural 1.

Bob was looking for the Garden of Plenty and had a map—the same map we had found on the dead body. It is said to be a safe haven with plenty of food. We will search for the Garden together with Bob.
