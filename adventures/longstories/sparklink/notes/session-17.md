# The Black Gem Cargo

## Arrival in Gracklstugh

We arrived in Gracklstugh and asked for directions to an inn at the harbor. Someone directed us toward Ghohlbrorn's Lair, in the Blade Bazaar.

On the way, an unseen figure bumped into Ulric. Ulric caught a brief shimmer and saw a gleaming purple psionic weapon on the figure as it moved toward the back of a harbor building. We followed it toward an abandoned alley. Ouus charged ahead. There, we saw someone picking up boxes. Ulric saw the shimmer approach the person from behind. We saved him from being stabbed by two psionic Duergar.

The person ran a "lucrative" business. As repayment, we asked for passage to the world above. He told us to meet him the next day at the Shattered Spire, pointing out a giant stalagmite near the docks.

The Duergar wore an insignia. Reference image filename: `a8b4997e-57d7-457b-9021-b733b6840159.png`. We saw the person working with boxes full of black gems, and Ulric took two of them.

## The Blade Bazaar and Ghohlbrorn's Lair

On the way to the inn, we passed several businesses:

- The Critter Menagerie (pet shop)
- Deepdelver's Collection (rare items)
- Enigam Emporeum (magic shop)
- Gable Shack (casino)
- Mushe's Spice and Everything Nice (herb shop)
- Envil's Edge (smith)
- Grand Bazaar (general store)

We got two rooms at Ghohlbrorn's Lair. Most of us ate; Amanita did not.

We learned that Duergar can turn invisible naturally, so guards may be unseen and illicit dealings require caution. We showed the barkeep the attackers' insignia and asked whether attacks were common. The barkeep warned us to be careful and more discreet, and said the Stone Guard might be able to help. Ulric asked about the Shattered Spire; the barkeep said it is a well-known tavern run by a nice but slightly odd man named Werz.

## What we miss about the world above

- **Ouus** misses the sun and his sister, As, who is also a soldier. They were close, and she is the main reason Ouus wants to return. Ouus worries about her, though she is not the worrying type and probably thinks he will be back in a few days. Ouus has often been the one to get her out of trouble. He wonders whether she will mature while he is away and no longer need an older brother.
- **Ulric** misses the light, the food, the freedom not to worry, and being among fellow halflings. Ulric does not have anyone important to miss.
- **Amanita** has no memories from the world above. Amanita's drive to go up comes from a fading memory: the wish to return to the family of the body's previous owner and tell them that their loved one is gone. Amanita now believes the girl seen in the lake's reflection was the previous owner's daughter and remembers that the previous owner loved her deeply. Ouus suggested the girl might have been her mother. Amanita cannot confirm or deny this because they do not know what that relationship feels like.
- **Skygge** was unusually quiet.
