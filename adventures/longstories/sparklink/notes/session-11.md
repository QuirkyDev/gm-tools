# The Shaman's Invitation

## Amanita's watch and the ambush

During Amanita's watch, Amanita noticed they were not alone and were being watched. Nearby were:

- Zerkwood mushrooms, which are branch-like.
- Whitecaps, which can "flower" and be used to make bread, pasta, and other food.
- Water orbs, described as fluid-filled sacks.

The creatures stalking us had purple skin and white hair. They were Drow.

Shoor Vandree ambushed us during our long rest. We barely survived, and Vaeril was nearly kidnapped.

## The Kuo-Toa by the dragon skull

We continued toward the druid. On the way, we passed a very rare dragon skull. Two Kuo-Toa were painting a skeleton with two heads and tentacles. They spoke almost obsessively about Leemooggoogoon, the Deep Father, a Kuo-Toa god we had never heard of. They worship him.

The Kuo-Toa live in the nearby city of Sloobludop and offered to take us there. They disliked Shuushar because she followed the Sea Mother. They also mentioned Blibdoolpoolp as their cult.

They said we should meet the shaman, who predicts that surface dwellers and their companions have a greater purpose. Perhaps we are part of a prophecy. To reach their village, we would travel beside the Dark Lake, which has a very open area.
