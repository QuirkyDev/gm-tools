# Following Droakie

## The Blade Bazaar

We returned to the Blade Bazaar and felt someone watching us. We encountered some invisible Stone Guard, but they weren't the ones watching. Amanita summoned a lizard to keep an eye out.

We spotted a man wearing a hat with two displacer beast tendrils. Amanita sent her lizard after him and followed at a distance. The man stopped at Mushrooms and Spice and Everything Nice, dropped off a package, and collected a bag of money without a word.

Amanita kept following him while Ouus and Ulric tried to steal the package. The Duergar behind the counter spotted them. Ouus ignored her and kept walking. Ulric flashed his Stone Guard insignia and ran off. She seemed relieved that they left without taking further action. Ulric took a good look in case he needed to identify her as a possible accomplice later. She wore her hair in a knot and had a distinctive robe that matched her hippie look.

## The Mushroom Deal

When Ouus and Ulric couldn't find Amanita, they opened the package. It contained two kinds of mushrooms, both with white stems. One had wide purple caps, and the other had narrow blue caps with white spots.

They returned to the shop and noticed that neither mushroom was on display. Ulric confronted the shopkeeper, who identified the purple mushrooms as **Bigwigs**, which make someone appear larger, and the blue ones as **Pygmywort**, which shrink a person's size.

The shopkeeper said both kinds were rare and hard to find, and she didn't normally sell them. She kept the dealings quiet because she didn't want to be associated with her supplier. When Ulric threatened to arrest her unless she cooperated, she confirmed the supplier was Droakie and said she didn't know where he lived. Buying mushrooms seemed to be her only connection to him.

She had paid 150 gp for the lot and planned to sell them for 25 gp apiece. We were impressed by the markup, which we put at about 566%. Ouus and Ulric extorted 200 gp from her in exchange for returning the mushrooms.

## The Sewers Below Gracklstugh

Amanita followed Droakie to the docks near where we'd encountered the assassins. Droakie disappeared into a sewer. Amanita investigated the area while her summoned lizard continued after him into a tunnel system beneath Gracklstugh. The tunnels held rare mushrooms unlike any Amanita had seen before, with the same wide purple caps and narrow, white-spotted blue caps as the mushrooms in the package.

Amanita reunited with the others and told them about the sewers and the mushrooms. They told her what had happened at the shop and split the money. Amanita returned to Mushrooms and Spice and Everything Nice and asked for a Pygmywort, which the shopkeeper gave her for free. We planned for Amanita to gather mushrooms for everyone.
