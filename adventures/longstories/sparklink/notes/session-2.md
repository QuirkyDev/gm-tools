# Northbound

## Choosing a route

Eldeth suggested heading north, toward Methozobara. The southern route passes through a darker area. The group chose to travel north.

## Party disagreement

Vaeril wanted to sell the item that creates the anti-magic field. Ouus wanted to throw it away because Artemas had stayed behind, outside the item's aura. Ouus promised to hold the item, but when Vaeril handed it to him, he threw it into the river.

## Discoveries and danger

The group found a map that may be a treasure map, marked with an X.

Artemas was ambushed by a ghost.
