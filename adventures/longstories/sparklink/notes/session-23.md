# The Voice in the Fissure

## The Sounds of Gracklstugh

A little farther on, we found a chamber with a plateau and cracks that gave off purple light. Amanita sent her lizard ahead to investigate. Through it she heard people talking and smiths at work. It sounded as if all of Gracklstugh could be heard in one place.

Ouus used Divine Sense. He sensed eight undead presences some distance away, a faint trace of undeath from Amanita, and ancient dark magic coming from the purple light. Amanita sent the lizard closer. It vanished with a pop.

Ouus approached the light himself and took no damage. He could hear the city more clearly than the lizard had, and he noticed that the light pulsed in time with the sounds. Amanita and Ulric joined him. As Amanita returned to her usual size, she heard a low voice.

## Red Scales in the Coins

Ulric suggested picking out one voice and listening to it. Amanita focused on the low voice and wondered where its owner was. A vision came to her. The speaker lived lavishly and earned gold for heating the furnaces, but he thought he deserved more. He believed he should rule Gracklstugh. Amanita caught his reflection in the coins and saw red scales.

## The Door on the Right

We followed a crack farther in and found a door on our right. We misjudged its weight and slammed it open. The noise drew several Duergar, who attacked us. We killed them. By the end of the fight, our spells were spent.

In a room on the left, among beds and desks, a Duergar woman was busy with her tools and alchemy. She had her headphones on and was listening to her iWizard. We decided to sneak up on her and knock her out. The session ended there, before we found out whether the plan would work.
