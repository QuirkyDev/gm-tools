# The Stone Guard's Assignment

## Ylsa's Offer

Ylsa offered to escort us with a caravan leaving in exactly seven days. Amanita proposed trading services and offered to protect the caravan on the road. Ylsa countered with an investigation. The Derro had been dealing in money from the surface, and she wanted us to find out where they were getting it. If we failed to complete the job, passage would cost 25 gp per person.

Ylsa described the Derro as smaller, more impulsive, and more solitary than the Duergar, and spoke of them as inferior. The Derro are oppressed and live in Laduguer's Furrow, a cavern outside the city walls. When Ylsa asked whether we had any other business, Amanita realized she was fishing for information she could use to her advantage. Ouus started to mention the Stone Guard, but Amanita cut him off, piquing Ylsa's interest.

Amanita asked Skygge whether he knew how to get home from the surface or was looking for another route. Skygge said he didn't know how to get home.

## Overlake Hold

At Overlake Hold, the Duergar who had fought alongside us welcomed us and introduced us to their commander, Errde Blackskull. They warned us that she was xenophobic and advised us to bow and address her as Commander. She asked how we had arrived and what we were doing in Gracklstugh. She was impressed when we told her we had tricked the drow and escaped captivity.

Blackskull offered us a paid assignment and free passage through Gracklstugh. She gave us four Stone Guard insignia to use during the assignment (image reference: `stoneguard_insignia.png`). She wanted us to find a Derro named Droakie who lives in the Furrow and had been seen in the Blade Bazaar. He had evaded capture and seemed to know the Stone Guard's patrol routes even when the guards were invisible. He wears a ragged imitation of a fancy jacket and a wide-brimmed hat adorned with two displacer beast tentacles.

Amanita asked what crimes Droakie had committed. Blackskull, who spoke of the Derro with even more contempt than Ylsa had, said he was suspected of conspiring with the Council of Savants and members of Gracklstugh's clan lairds to overthrow the Deep King and his regime. The Council comprises 36 elected Derro savants who live in greater comfort. Blackskull characterized that privilege as a gift from the Duergar.

Completing the assignment would earn us 200 gp, and we might be allowed to keep the insignia permanently. Blackskull advised us to visit the stone giants as soon as possible.

## Laduguer's Furrow and the Caregorn Caverns

At the gates, two Duergar appeared out of invisibility and told us to leave. When we showed our insignia, they used their size-changing ability and let us pass.

Laduguer's Furrow is a chasm about 20 metres deep, crossed by numerous bridges. Paths and homes are carved into its sides, and we could see and hear a community of about a thousand Derro. On the far side, houses were built against rocky pillars, with shops clustered around them in groups of three to five.

In the Caregorn Caverns, Dahrun spotted us and took us to the Stone Speaker Hgraam, who was meditating. Dahrun helped us climb the giant-sized stairs. Hgraam had received a cryptic message from the stones that had yet to be deciphered and was likely connected to what had happened to Rihuus (Rihhuut).

> Something evil stirs in the Underdark. The rock itself cries in pain and horror, and a madness creeps from the blackest depths. Pay heed to the signs surrounding you. A cave with two faces. Rock devoured, and the land overgrown. The pebble believes itself flesh. The earth rejects its wards, and the tunnels shake in fury. By these portents, you shall know of evil’s presence and of evil’s face.

Hgraam also had a predominantly purple vision. He couldn't identify what he saw, as he could with something familiar like amethyst, and it appeared to spread throughout the Underdark. While speaking to the stones, he felt something try to invade him and felt pain in his neck where Rihuus had grown his second head.

As a token of appreciation, Hgraam gave us a magical crystal the size of a halfling's forearm. He said it could “speak to you as Skoraeus Stonebones wills it.” Skoraeus Stonebones is the god of buried things and the King of the Rock.
