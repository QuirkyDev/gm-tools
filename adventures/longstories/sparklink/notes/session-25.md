# A Concept of a Plan

## Reporting to Commander Blackskull

We returned to the Stone Guard and asked for an audience with Commander Errde Blackskull. We told her what we'd learned about the criminal organization and Droakie. As soon as we handed her Gorglak's letter, she ordered her guard to arrest us. We quickly convinced her we could help. Gorglak would see the Stone Guard coming, but he'd never met us.

Blackskull told us Gorglak would be on duty at the northern gate the next day with three other guards. We could recognize him by his curled moustache and Stone Guard uniform. The Duergar collected exotic weapons and currently carried an elven blade.

Blackskull identified Lorthia's organization as the Grey Ghosts. Gundren ran the laboratory, but Brakka Ironshade had led the Grey Ghosts. About a week ago, the Keepers of the Flame had executed Brakka in secret.

Blackskull offered two additional rewards. She'd pay **100 gp** if we brought Droakie in alive, or **25 gp** if we brought him in dead. Further investigation into the Grey Ghosts would earn us another **100 gp**.

## Papers at the Northern Gate

Back at Ghohlbrorn's Lair, we took a long rest and planned our ambush. We'd talk to Gorglak, try to get the other guards on our side, and ambush him if he slipped up.

The next day, Gorglak asked for our papers. That was when we realized we had more of a concept of a plan.

Skygge asked Gorglak for a word in private, and he came with us. Amanita asked about his sword. Gorglak grew nervous and defensive and said he'd bought it at Anvil's Edge. Ouus started reminiscing about his uncle and told the story of how he'd been a turncoat in the military.

Gorglak tried to call the other guards. Skygge cast *Hold Person*, but the spell failed. Amanita cast *Faerie Fire*.
