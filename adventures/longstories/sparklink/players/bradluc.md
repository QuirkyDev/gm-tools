# Bradluc


## Session 10: Meet Bradluc and Voc

Bradluc joined the fight against the gnolls with the steel defender Voc. Bradluc does not speak Common, but speaks Elvish and Dwarvish, and is also heading to Timberbark Grove to seek a cure for Drow poison. Bradluc identified the pin as an insignia of House Vandree and recognized the shrine as a gnoll shrine. In the oasis pool, Bradluc saw the woman Bradluc is dating, cast *Detect Magic*, and sensed divination magic. Bradluc cast *Alarm* before the group spent the night.

## Session 12: The city with two fishy shamans

Bradluc listened to the Kuo-Toa as the group walked (Perception 20). Bradluc later spoke with the second Kuo-Toa group and managed to avoid conflict.

## Session 14: The Druid's Cure

Voc, Bradluc's steel defender, helped serve as bait to catch the Spellsip Suckers.

## Session 15: The Return of Skygge

Bradluc asked Celyn for a recipe for an antidote to Drow poison. The group agreed to drop Bradluc off on the way to Gracklstugh. Bradluc was charmed by the harpies' singing.

## Session 16: Sailing into Gracklstugh

Bradluc told the group to visit Glamrock Hold if they were nearby; it is mostly inhabited by deep gnomes, and they should ask for Bradluc or a guild whose name the group could not remember. Bradluc rode Voc off into the sunset.
