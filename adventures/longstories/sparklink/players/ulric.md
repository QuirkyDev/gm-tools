# Ulric


## Session 5: Entering the Garden of Plenty

After eating the garden's food and drinking wine, Ulric spaced out completely.

## Session 7: The Six Awakened

Ulric noticed the old Elvish names beneath the statues after rolling 15 on a Perception check.

## Session 9: A Night at the Drowsy Puffball

Ulric lent Vaeril seven Drossle to pay for the night at the Drowsy Puffball.

## Session 10: Meet Bradluc and Voc

Ulric confirmed that there are no hyena-like creatures with a third eye above ground. In the oasis pool, Ulric saw guards approaching and turned away. Ulric would rather have left the chamber, but the group spent the night there.

## Session 15: The Return of Skygge

Ulric was not charmed by the harpies' singing and tried unsuccessfully to counter the charm.

## Session 16: Sailing into Gracklstugh

Ulric said he never knew a father and does not know whether his parents died. Ulric was raised in an orphanage, moved around a lot, and found his patron more recently.

## Session 17: The Black Gem Cargo

Ulric was bumped by an unseen figure and briefly saw it shimmer while carrying a purple psionic weapon. Ulric later saw it approach a person in an alley, helped save that person from two psionic Duergar, and took two black gems from the boxes. Ulric misses the light, food, freedom from worry, and being among fellow halflings.

## Session 18: The Shattered Spire's Offer

Ulric asked what everyone wanted to become as a child. Ulric wanted enough money to live comfortably and help other orphans.
