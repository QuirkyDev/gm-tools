# Artemas


## Session 2: Northbound

Artemas stayed behind, outside the anti-magic field item's aura. He was later ambushed by a ghost.

## Session 3: The Gnoll Chase

The ghost-like creature drained some of Artemas's strength, leaving him unable to carry some of his items. Ouus offered to carry Artemas's shield and crowbar.

## Session 6: Echoes of the Six Sisters

Artemas kept watch over the place where the zombies had come from.

## Session 7: The Six Awakened

Artemas was killed by the undead leader, who emerged from the coffin and killed him with a single hit. Amanita later cast *Gentle Repose* on him.

## Session 8: Leaving the Garden

The group learned that a druid at Timberbark Grove may be able to revive Artemas.

## Session 14: The Druid's Cure

The group left Artemas at the hut with Caelith, the druid, while they went to gather Spellsip Suckers for her antidote.

## Session 15: The Return of Skygge

Artemas awakened after Caelith's resurrection ritual but seemed to have amnesia. The last thing he remembered was seeking treasure for the Raven Queen: a knife. He said his name was Skygge, and the group wondered whether someone who had previously inhabited Artemas's body had returned. Caelith said that whatever had controlled Skygge's body had been cleansed. Skygge agreed to join the search for a way out of the Underdark.

## Session 16: Sailing into Gracklstugh

Skygge said he is loyal to the Raven Queen. In his society, clans care for one another instead of having parental relationships. Skygge wants to find a portal home before meeting the Raven Queen.

## Session 17: The Black Gem Cargo

Skygge was unusually quiet during the conversation about what the party misses about the world above.

## Session 18: The Shattered Spire's Offer

Skygge said individual dreams were a strange concept. Skygge lives for the clan and its goals, and wants to follow the Raven Queen, find her artifacts, and make her stronger.

## Session 19: The Malformed Giant

Skygge looked over the games at the Gable Shack without trying to be addressed.
