# Vaeril


## Session 1: Prisoners of the Underdark

Vaeril overheard Shoor and Ilvara speaking. A woman in the conversation said she did not want to be there, did not understand why her father had put her there, and believed the situation was connected to political struggles. The notes do not make clear which woman this refers to.

## Session 2: Northbound

Vaeril wanted to sell the item that creates the anti-magic field. After Ouus promised to hold it, Vaeril handed it to him; Ouus then threw it into the river.

## Session 4: Gnolls in the Dark

Vaeril explored part of the cave, heard sounds that might have come from hostiles, and pointed out possible hostile locations to the group.

## Session 5: Entering the Garden of Plenty

Vaeril practiced "Mask of the Wild" and can now use it around mushrooms. Vaeril copied down the temple's elvish-like writing but could not read it. Vaeril later investigated a sound alone and saw strange figures, some apparently missing parts, approaching the temple, then called to Ouus that some of his friends had arrived.
