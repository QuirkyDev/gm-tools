# Amanita


## Session 5: Entering the Garden of Plenty

Amanita went mushroom-picking, noticed Zerkwood, bluecaps, and whitecaps, and stopped early because the activity was disturbing others. Amanita also told the group that they had lost all their memories.

## Session 6: Echoes of the Six Sisters

Amanita kept watch over the place where the zombies had come from.

## Session 7: The Six Awakened

Amanita heard what sounded like someone pounding on a wall. After the fight, Amanita took all seven identical rings bearing a flame symbol, then learned *Gentle Repose* and cast it on Artemas.

## Session 9: A Night at the Drowsy Puffball

Amanita spoke telepathically with the slimes. They seemed to believe Amanita was their "master" (uncertain) and asked whether Amanita needed more of them. Amanita said no more were needed and told them to wait one day before going to Blingdenstone.

## Session 10: Meet Bradluc and Voc

Amanita picked up the dark-metal-and-gold pin. Amanita spoke with Bradluc in Elvish, saw a ten-year-old human girl in the oasis pool and felt deep love without recognizing her, reached into the water without effect, and spent the night staring into it.

## Session 11: The Shaman's Invitation

During Amanita's watch, Amanita noticed they were being watched by Drow with purple skin and white hair. Amanita also noted the nearby Zerkwood mushrooms, Whitecaps, and water orbs.

## Session 15: The Return of Skygge

Amanita related their own experience to Artemas's: Amanita believes they did not inhabit this body before waking as Amanita. Caelith sensed Amanita's essence and confirmed Amanita is their own person, likely a sentient spore servant whose previous host is no longer present. Amanita was not charmed by the harpies and tried to bonk Ouus on the head, but failed.

## Session 16: Sailing into Gracklstugh

Amanita steered the boat. Amanita accidentally pushed Ouus into the water with *Thunderwave* during the fight against the Duergar and felt bad about it. Amanita shared that they do not know the parents of the body they inhabit, but Caelith helped them learn they have a myconid creator whom they want to meet. Amanita recalled the young girl from the lake's reflection and thinks it may have been a memory of the body's previous inhabitant.

## Session 17: The Black Gem Cargo

Amanita did not eat at Ghohlbrorn's Lair. Amanita wants to reach the world above because of a fading memory of returning to the previous owner's family and telling them their loved one is gone. Amanita believes the girl seen in the lake's reflection was the previous owner's daughter, whom that person loved deeply. Ouus suggested the girl might have been the previous owner's mother; Amanita cannot confirm or deny this.

## Session 18: The Shattered Spire's Offer

Amanita was broke at first, then bought 10 gp hide armor from the Iron Stitch and sold the old armor. Amanita said they do not yet have a big dream beyond growing stronger and reaching the surface. Amanita used a stick to hit two aggressive figures after they touched them.

## Session 19: The Malformed Giant

Amanita went to the bar at the Gable Shack, asked for Ylsa, explained that the group needed help traveling, and ordered drinks.
