# Ouus


## Session 1: Prisoners of the Underdark

Ouus stole drow poison from Ilvara's room during the most recent work detail and concealed how he did it.

## Session 2: Northbound

Ouus wanted to throw away the item that creates the anti-magic field because Artemas had stayed behind, outside its aura. He promised Vaeril he would hold it, but threw it into the river after Vaeril handed it to him.

## Session 3: The Gnoll Chase

Ouus offered to carry Artemas's shield and crowbar after a ghost-like creature drained some of Artemas's strength. During the combat to help Bob escape the gnolls, Ouus gained inspiration after rolling a natural 1.

## Session 4: Gnolls in the Dark

Ouus went on the offensive after Vaeril pointed out possible hostile locations. The group killed several gnolls, though their leader escaped.

## Session 5: Entering the Garden of Plenty

Ouus sparred with Suortin and felt at home in the ruined temple. Vaeril later called out to Ouus that some of his friends had arrived.

## Session 6: Echoes of the Six Sisters

Ouus told the princess about the zombies, but she did not care because she was too high.

## Session 7: The Six Awakened

Ouus noticed the old Elvish names beneath the statues after rolling 15 on a Perception check. Ouus took the sword from the grave; it was inscribed, “I isilrîn le govannen” (“Those who freeze, greet you”).

## Session 8: Leaving the Garden

Ouus finally snapped Vaeril out of the feeling that he never wanted to leave the Garden of Plenty.

## Session 10: Meet Bradluc and Voc

Ouus attacked the three gnolls at the rubble pile, beginning combat. At the gnoll shrine, Ouus sensed that evil rituals had been cast there. In the oasis pool, Ouus briefly saw Artemas before the vision changed into something powerful and unfamiliar; Ouus turned away.
